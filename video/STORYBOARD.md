# Agora Virtual Estates: promo storyboard

**Format:** 1920 × 1080 (16:9), 30 fps, about 70 seconds. Every frame is drawn in code: no stock footage, no screenshots.

## What we borrow from the reference (style only)

The reference is a 60-second, 16:9 launch film for a different product. We take its rhythm, not its assets:

- **Title cards alternate with UI moments.** A short typewriter title card (about 2–3 s, with a blinking caret) sets up each idea. A 3–6 s product moment shows it.
- **Typography.** Each title is a big, tight headline with one key word styled differently. In our version, the headline is Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold in ink, and the key word is Agora blue with a sun-yellow highlighter swipe.
- **Soft backgrounds.** The background is off-white with large blurred colour blobs in the corners. Ours are blue-tint, sun-tint and grape-tint.
- **Camera.** The app window floats in 3D perspective, drifts slowly, and pushes in close on the detail that matters.
- **Transitions.** Hard cuts land on the beat, colour bars sweep across between acts, and the film ends on a calm logo card with a tagline.

The brand adds what the reference doesn't have:
- 3 px ink outlines and hard offset shadows on every card
- the mascot as the hero character, acting out every beat

## Notes

- All copy, timings and numbers live in a single content file (`src/content.ts`), so text changes don't need any animation code edited.
- Property facts come from the project's sample data. The EMI is computed from the same defaults the app uses: 35% down, 7.75%, 20 years.
- The mascot drawing is a snapshot of `apps/web/src/ui/mascot/art.tsx`, re-posed for the film. The app's own files are untouched.
- **Audio:** silent by default. Optionally, an original music bed generated in code (no samples, no downloads), added as a separate track.
